Ph.D. Candidate, KAIST

I explore how to make inaccessible sensory information perceivable and actionable.

My research combines cross-modal interaction, multisensory XR, and human–AI interfaces to help people perceive, share, and create digital experiences across XR, AR, mobile, and wearable platforms.
